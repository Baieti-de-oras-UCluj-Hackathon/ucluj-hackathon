# UmbraRo Product Specification

## 1. Product Overview

UmbraRo is a premium AI tactical assistant for professional football coaches and technical staff.

It is not:
- a betting app
- a fan app
- a fantasy football product
- a generic score-tracking platform

UmbraRo is a football intelligence and decision-support system built on top of a machine learning backend.

Its purpose is to support:
- prediction
- explanation
- prescription

## 2. Academic Context

UmbraRo is the practical product built for the thesis:

**UmbraRo: A Model-Based Tactical Blueprint System for Romanian Superliga Football Management**

The thesis argues that:
- predictive analytics explains what is likely to happen
- prescriptive analytics explains what should be done
- coaching workflows need more than pure forecasting
- UmbraRo bridges machine learning and practical football decision support

## 3. Target Users

Primary users:
- head coaches
- assistant coaches
- tactical analysts
- technical staff

Secondary users:
- performance and match-preparation staff

## 4. Core Product Pillars

### Prediction
Estimate the probability of a home win for a fixture.

### Explanation
Show the main tactical and contextual drivers behind the probability.

### Prescription
Recommend realistic tactical target values that improve the chance of success.

## 5. Core Screens

- Dashboard / Home
- Standings
- Match Intelligence
- Tactical Blueprint
- Analytics
- Command Chat
- Team / Squad

## 6. Product Tone

UmbraRo should feel:
- elite
- sharp
- tactical
- analytical
- premium
- serious
- professional

It must not feel:
- playful
- social
- betting-oriented
- fan-oriented
- generic

## 7. Product Principles

Every screen should help answer at least one question:
- What is our chance of winning?
- Why is that the current probability?
- What tactical conditions would improve it?
- What should the staff do next?

## 8. Constraints

- The product must stay grounded in the actual backend.
- Do not invent unsupported data.
- Do not add fake advanced football metrics.
- Do not change the binary target formulation unless explicitly instructed.
